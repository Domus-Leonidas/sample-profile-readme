## 🚀 Founder & Developer | Leontheon

### [Leontheon.com](https://leontheon.com)

I founded Leontheon with the belief that technology should simplify people's lives without compromising their privacy, independence, or control over personal information.

My vision is to develop intelligent systems that help individuals and families organize information, manage responsibilities, make informed decisions, and preserve meaningful knowledge over time.

At its core, Leontheon is guided by a few principles:

- **Clarity:** Technology should make information easier to understand and decisions easier to navigate.
- **Responsibility:** Intelligent systems should operate within clearly defined boundaries, with accountability and human oversight.
- **Privacy & Consent:** People should retain meaningful control over their personal information, identities, and permissions.
- **Trust:** Security, transparency, and reliability should be built into technology from the beginning.
- **Continuity:** Important information, knowledge, and responsibilities should be preserved for the people authorized to receive them, including across generations.

I want Leontheon to demonstrate that advanced technology and responsible design can coexist. Artificial intelligence should support human judgment, not replace personal autonomy.
